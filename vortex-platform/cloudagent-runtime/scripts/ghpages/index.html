FAQ

Straight answers

Is there a free trial or free plan?

No free trial — the builder is a serious production tool. Instead you get Big Billion Day: on the 1st of EVERY month, 8:00 AM – 12:00 PM IST, the whole platform opens FREE for 4 hours with Pro-level power. No card, no commitment.

Do I need my own AI API key?

No. The builder ships with built-in AI including open-source models running on our servers — you can create apps from minute one without a single API key. Power users can still attach their own providers (OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, Groq, Ollama…) to run at their own rates.

How do payments work?

All billing runs through Razorpay: UPI (PhonePe / Google Pay / Paytm), debit/credit cards and netbanking. Monthly plans activate instantly after payment and can be cancelled anytime from your account.

Do you offer lifetime access?

No — deliberately. CloudAgent is a living product: new models, new toolchains and new features ship every month. Every plan is a rolling 30-day subscription with no lock-in; cancel and renew as you wish.

Can I get a self-hosted / source license?

Yes — businesses can license CloudAgent with source for on-premise deployment on an annual agreement. Contact us with your use case and team size for a quote.

Ready to build at agent speed?
